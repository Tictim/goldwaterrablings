Today I birth into existence.

It feels quite surreal to think my first rodeo into a "proper" creation, one that I can semi-proudly put my name on, has happened in a place I know so little about: A goddamn website. I mean, I can code, I can even bang out some easy shaders, like the water you see in the background. Water, say hi! _<span style="color: #5087cc">(hi!)</span>_

The act of creation, the concept, evokes me an implication of some sort of solemnity. The act of creation, where, a being from materials rises, a life from mundanity bears; the kind of act you are not allowed to even mutter while you look.

In contrast, the experience of making this website has been nothing but clanking noises and angry shoutings. All I had was basic understanding of Vue.js, some coding ability, and free time. During the making of this website I had to learn VitePress, learn to write SVG, [build a tool for making gradient map filter in SVG](/tools/gradient) because holy shit some of the things in SVG are literally unusable. Basically nothing displayed here is envisioned nor planned. I didn't have a website name until like last week. Big inspiration of the website's design came from [A. M. Cassandre's poster arts](https://www.cassandre-france.com/poster-gallery-by-cassandre) and its aesthetics, an inspiration that has largely evaporated as I realized I just couldn't.

I had a small goal of finishing the website before and publishing it at my birthday. So that I can celebrate myself without being weird... I just celebrate my website's instead and get indirect fulfillment! Little did I know, my cute little plan ended up almost driving me to meltdown from all the pressure. Every information [Inigo Quilez](https://iquilezles.org/) has put on the Internet has been a lifeboat. Also shoutouts to Mark Jarzynski and Marc Olano, for their phenomenal work on _[Hash Functions for GPU Rendering](https://jcgt.org/published/0009/03/02/)_. Without them and countless faceless heroes, who basically hand-fed me the barest of basics, I don't know if I could have made it on time.

I think making this website changed my view on some of things. Now when I think of art-making, before the vague notion of solemnity, I get reminded of the visceral experience I had. An absolute shitshow where I pretend to have a vision, wrestle both my lacking ability and knowledge, and fight the woefully inadequate documentations of Three.js. Dear fucking god why is the default color space of textures linearRGB? When I found out the color of SVG images and canvas didn't match even remotely, I felt myself physically sinking. It's over. I'll never have this done in this year. Or next year. Or next next next next next next next next next. Why are you doing this? What do you need your website for? [Your one and only incoherent "essay" you yourself feel too lazy to revise?](/xeno3) You gonna put me on your portfolio? Why are you doing this?

Why am I doing this?

Video game as an entertainment form is largely commercial in nature. A commercially driven art has many shared attributes. Most notable would be its reason-to-exist, or the lack of. Commercial incentives, even at small dose, define the project from its core; how it is conceptualized, how it is made, how it lives on, etc.. A commercial project does not need an exact "why" on its existence. Do you think they are remaking this boomer game to relay its legacy to generations of tomorrow?

I analyze video games as a hobby. And with video games being commercial entertainments, I tend to just take their existence as granted. I don't really have to ask why a game came into being, I just play. Actually I take most things in life as granted, silly me. But during the making of this website I had to ask myself the question on why. Why make this? What do I get out of this? Beside a website.

After finishing the job, I can proudly say that I don't know! I have no idea. But one thing I now know is that it is me. Me. This website exists because it is me. It had to exist because I am incomplete without. Through the act of creation I birth into existence. Look at me! See the baby's first shader, see the terrible glassslop UI! Look at me, see my unfunny jokes! And maybe laugh, maybe not! Look, look, look at me! _<span style="color: #5087cc">(look at me!)</span>_

Look at [me](/me)!
